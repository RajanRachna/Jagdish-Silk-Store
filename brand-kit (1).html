Brand Voice

Warm, a little playful, with light Punjabi ("Punglish") mixed into English — never full translation, just flavour where it feels natural.

Use Punjabi words for greetings, celebration, and warmth. Keep functional text (buttons, form labels, instructions) in plain, clear English.
